## What you get

### Speak, don't spec

Describe your project in plain English. No pin numbers, no datasheets, no googling "ESP32 GPIO 14 can I use PWM?"

### See exactly what connects where

Clear wiring diagrams showing every connection. Plus step-by-step assembly instructions so you never wonder "did I wire this right?"

### Works with what you have

Arduino Uno, ESP32, Raspberry Pi Pico — works with the boards you already own. Supports hundreds of common sensors, displays, and modules.

### Flash in one click

Direct deployment to your board via PlatformIO. From description to running hardware in under a minute.

### Built for makers, not engineers

No electrical engineering degree required. If you can describe what you want to build, Schematik handles the rest. Hardware is for everyone now.

### Export and tinker

Download complete code, Fritzing schematics, and assembly guides. Modify, learn, customize. Make it truly yours.
